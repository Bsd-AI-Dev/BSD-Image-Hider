Private Image Hider – How to use

Images on websites are replaced by a grey striped box. The page layout stays the same, so websites keep working. You decide where images are shown.

Tip: pin the extension so its button is always visible. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to Private Image Hider.

1. Show a single image

Move the mouse over a hidden image. A small 👁 appears in its corner. Click it to show that image. Click ↺ to hide it again.

Keyboard: hover the image and press Alt+A to show it, Alt+Z to hide it.

2. The toolbar button

Click the extension’s button to see what’s happening on the current page and change it.

ButtonWhat it does
Show images on whole siteImages are shown on every page of this website.
Show images on this page onlyImages are shown on just this page. The rest of the site stays hidden.
Keep hiding images on this pageAppears when the whole site is set to show images. Keeps images hidden on this one page anyway.
Hide small icons & logos tooNormally tiny images (icons, buttons, logos) are left alone. Turn this on to hide them too.
Pause this tabShows all images in this tab until you unpause it or close the tab.
Show all images this timeShows everything on this page until you reload or leave it.
Pause everywhereTurns hiding off in every tab until you unpause.

The box at the top of the popup tells you whether images are hidden or shown on the page, and which rule decided it.

Clicking a button marked ✓ again undoes it.

3. Several pages at once: the Settings page

Open it from the popup (Manage lists & settings…). There are two lists, one entry per line:

  • Show images on… sites and pages where images are shown.
  • Always hide images on… pages where images stay hidden, even if the whole site is in the first list.

How to write an entry

You typeIt covers
contoso.comThe whole site, including subdomains like shop.contoso.com
contoso.com/home.htmlExactly that one page. Endings like ?ref=email or #contact are ignored.
contoso.com/Only the site’s front page
contoso.com/blog/*Every page whose address starts with contoso.com/blog/
recipesAny address containing the word “recipes”

You can paste full addresses (https://www.contoso.com/home.html). They are tidied up when you save.

Which rule wins?

  1. If the page matches Always hide → images are hidden.
  2. Otherwise, if it matches Show images on → images are shown.
  3. Otherwise → images are hidden (the default).

4. Small images

By default, images smaller than 48 pixels stay visible so buttons and menus stay usable. On the Settings page you can change that size, or tick Hide small icons and logos too to hide everything.

Not covered: icons drawn with special fonts (they’re text, not images) and the little site icon on the browser tab.

Good to know

  • Images still download in the background; they’re just not shown. This doesn’t save data.
  • On some pages an image may flash for a split second while the page loads.
  • Extensions can’t run on the browser’s own pages (settings, the extension store, the new tab page).
  • If a page looks wrong, use Pause this tab or Show all images this time.

Privacy

This extension has no network access, no analytics and no remote code. Your settings and lists are stored only in this browser and never leave it.
